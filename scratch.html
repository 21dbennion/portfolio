Coldplay has been my favorite band for a long time. I can still remember the first songs I ever listened to by them: Clocks and Paradise.

Why I Love Coldplay

  1. Variety
    • No matter how you are feeling, there is a Coldplay song for that moment
    • Some songs are very upbeat, while others are thoughtful and peaceful
  2. They make music that makes you feel something
  3. Some bands are known for their storytelling, but Coldplay tells the story through how the song makes you feel
